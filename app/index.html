    Your edits stay on this device.

    What it does

    1. Cuts dead air between phrases and keeps your natural beats.
    2. Finds retakes, stutters, and fillers for you to review.
    3. Captions every word in the house style: 1–3 word chunks in a dark box.
    4. Reframes to 9:16 with punch-ins on the cuts so the frame keeps moving.
    5. Coaches the story: hook, pace, ending, and safe zones.

    Video never leaves your device. Only speech audio goes to the transcription service you choose, with your own key.
